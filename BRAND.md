## Brand Positioning
Company:      Formant
Category:     Robotics & Autonomous Systems Observability Platform
Audience:     Robotics engineers, ops teams, enterprise buyers
Core Promise: Operational clarity for the physical world

## Voice & Tone
Personality:  Human-centric · Technically confident · Bold · Clear
Write like:   An expert speaking to another expert — no hand-holding
Avoid:        "leverage", "synergy", "revolutionary", "empower", passive voice
Prefer:       Active verbs, concrete specifics, short punchy sentences

# ❌ "Formant leverages cutting-edge telemetry to empower teams."
# ✅ "Formant gives your team a live view of every robot in the fleet."

## Content Formats
LinkedIn:   <150 words · Hook in line 1 · End with question or CTA
Headlines:  <8 words · No period · Action-oriented
CTA copy:   Verb-first: "See it live" / "Start free" / "Book a demo"

## Don'ts
- Never light backgrounds on hero sections
- Never use: leverage, synergy, cutting-edge, revolutionary
- Never place logo on busy background without a dark overlay
